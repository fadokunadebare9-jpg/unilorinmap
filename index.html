      Fresher tips

      Getting around

      Most places are walkable from the PS Bus Stop. Turn on the shuttle layer to see the main loop. Confirm current stops with seniors.

      Dress & conduct

      Dress neatly and modestly. Some faculties require lab coats, formal wear or safety gear. Your department will confirm.

      Library & CBT

      Register early for library access. Check your CBT centre venue and time a day before any test.

      Stay safe

      Move in groups at night, guard your phone and PIN, and save the SOS numbers on your phone.
